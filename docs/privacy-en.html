Ski Info privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ski Info is a free app for looking up ski resort information (runs, lifts, altitude, piste map and weather forecast). This page explains what data the app handles and how.

No accounts, no personal data

Ski Info needs no sign-up, has no user accounts and does not collect, store or share personally identifiable data (name, email, contacts, etc.), except what you choose to send us with the feedback form. It shows no ads.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and improve it, Ski Info records anonymous usage statistics:

  • A random identifier created on your device the first time you open the app and stored only there. It is not linked to your name, account, phone or any advertising identifier.
  • What you do in the app: when you open it, which resorts you look at, whether you open the map or tap the accommodation link, how long you use it, whether you use the Android app or a browser, which site you came from and your device's time zone (which gives the approximate country without using your IP address).

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), acting as storage provider. Only the person responsible for Ski Info can access it; it is used solely to measure how the app is used, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is deleted after 13 months. As it isn't tied to your identity, we cannot tell which records are yours; if you clear the app's data (or uninstall it), a new identifier is created.

The Android app also includes Firebase Analytics (Google) for basic install and usage statistics (first open, sessions), with advertising-ID collection turned off.

Your location

Ski Info can use your location for two things, always only with your permission: showing the resorts near you and showing where you are on the piste map.

  • Your location is used only on your device. It is never sent to any server or included in the statistics.
  • For nearby resorts, a rounded position (to roughly one kilometre) is kept on your device. Your position on the map is not stored.
  • Without that permission, nearby resorts are worked out from your device's time zone, and everything else works the same.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in your browser or phone settings.

Data the app loads

To work, the app makes requests to these services, which receive no personal data from you: they only serve content or answer for the location of the resort you are viewing, not yours:

  • OpenStreetMap / OpenSkiMap: run, lift and resort data (ODbL licence).
  • Open-Meteo: weather and forecast for the coordinates of the resort you are viewing.
  • Esri / Maxar / Earthstar Geographics: satellite imagery for the piste map.

Requests go straight from your device to each service. Ski Info has no server of its own: it is a static website.

Links to other sites (Booking.com and official websites)

Each resort page includes external links, such as its official website or an accommodation search on Booking.com. When you tap them you are on that third party's site, under its own privacy policy. Ski Info receives no data about you or your bookings.

Some links may be affiliate links (run by networks such as CJ or Awin): if you book after tapping one, Ski Info may earn a commission at no cost to you. To attribute the booking, the affiliate network records the click and may use cookies, under its own policy. Affiliate links are marked as such in the app.

Feedback form

If you write to us from “Send feedback” (or “Report a data error”, or from a route), we receive by email your message, the page you write from, the language, whether you use the website or the app and, only if you give it, your email address. It is sent through Web3Forms, which acts as a provider and forwards the message to us. We use this only to read your feedback and, if you leave your email, to reply; we do not use it for anything else or share it. You can ask us to delete your message by writing to the contact address.

Local storage

The app keeps on your own device the statistics identifier, your saved resorts, your chosen language and display preferences (such as the map's colour mode). None of it leaves your device, and it is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

App permissions (Android)

The Android app asks for internet access, needed to load the data, and, only if you allow it, your location, for what is described under “Your location”. It does not ask for camera, contacts, storage or any other sensitive permission.

Children

Ski Info is not aimed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect data from children, as it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app works changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated with the new date.
